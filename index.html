You're not lazy. You're overloaded.

Most apps add another list, another streak, another reason to feel behind. Tula does the opposite. It holds the details so your head doesn't have to, and it plans for the person you are today.

A calmer day in four steps

  1. Dump it all

    Type everything messy in one go. Tula turns it into tasks with dates, stakes and size. You confirm before anything is saved.

  2. Check in

    Mood, energy, stress. Ten seconds. Tula reads how much you have in the tank today.

  3. Get 2 or 3 picks

    Not 40 tasks. Just what fits your energy and what can't wait, each with a reason why.

  4. Let it hold the rest

    Every deadline in the next 72 hours stays on the radar. Nothing quietly disappears.

What Tula will never do

No streaks, no guilt

Missing a day doesn't reset anything. Rest is part of the plan, not a failure of it.

No silent changes

Tula suggests. You decide. It never adds, moves or deletes your commitments without asking.

No pretending

It tells you what it knows, what it's guessing, and what it can't do yet. No fake data, no fake actions.

No notification spam

Few nudges, quiet hours, and every reminder easy to pause.

Why Tula exists

"Missed assignments. Internship deadlines. A missed offer. There were too many apps, and none of them were a real solution."

Tula is being built by a student who grows three tech communities alongside college, and kept watching important things slip. It's built in the open and tested on real, messy weeks.

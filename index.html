Deadlines don't arrive in one place.

The lab record notice, buried under 200 messages
The internship form that closed last night
The extension announced in the other group
The fee date on a PDF nobody opened

You didn't forget. It was never in one place.

Calendars and to-do apps wait for you to type things in. College doesn't work like that. Notices arrive mid-semester, change twice, and show up in three groups. Tula is built for that mess.

From a forwarded notice to done

  1. Send the notice

    Paste a message from your class group, or type it your way. Tula pulls out the date, what to submit and the link. You check it before it saves.

  2. One record per deadline

    Extended to Monday? Same notice in another group? Tula updates the one record and shows you what changed.

  3. Reminders by stakes

    Three days, one day and the morning of for forms and offers. A day and three hours before for everything else.

  4. See what's due

    Each day, the 2 or 3 things to act on, plus everything due in the next 72 hours.

Built for what happens after you save it

Saving a deadline is the easy part. Tula does the part that comes after.

Follows extensions

When a date moves, Tula keeps the history, so you always know which date is the real one.

Catches repeats

The same notice from three groups stays one deadline, not three.

Reminders by stakes

An internship form gets more warning than a class quiz, because missing it costs more.

What's due, every day

2 or 3 picks and a 72-hour radar. Snoozing something never takes it off the radar.

Beyond coursework

Internships, hackathons, scholarships and fees sit beside your assignments.

Never guesses a date

Tula shows the words the date came from. If a notice says "soon", it asks you instead of guessing.

Coming next

Android app

Share a message, screenshot or PDF from any app straight to Tula, with reminders that reach you when the app is closed. In this early web version you paste the text, and reminders show while the page is open.

What Tula will never do

Never read your chats

Tula sees only what you send it. No scanning groups, no reading notifications.

Never change things silently

It suggests, you confirm. It never adds, moves or deletes a deadline without your tap.

Never pretend

It tells you what it read, what it's unsure of, and what it can't do yet. No fake actions.

Never keep what you want gone

Delete everything in one tap, any time.

Why Tula exists

"Missed assignments. Internship deadlines. A missed offer. There were too many apps, and none of them were a real solution."

Tula is being built by a student who grows three tech communities alongside college, and kept watching important things slip. It's built in the open and tested on real, messy weeks.

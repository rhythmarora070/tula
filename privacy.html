Privacy policy

Last updated 12 November 2026. Tula is for college students aged 18 and over.

In short: Tula keeps only the deadline details you confirm. It never reads your chats. Your deadlines live on your phone unless you sign in to back them up. If you use AI reading, a notice you share is sent once to read the deadline and is not kept. You can delete everything in one tap.

Tula does not collect your contacts, location, call logs or chats, and contains no advertising or third-party tracking code.

Notices you share

When you share a message, screenshot or PDF to Tula, the app reads it to find the deadline. Without an account, this happens on your phone and only text can be read. With an account, the notice is sent once over an encrypted connection to our server, which asks Google's Gemini API to read it. We ask Google not to store the request, our server does not save or log the notice, and the screenshot or PDF is not kept by the app. Only the fields you confirm are saved.

Notices often contain other people's names or numbers. Because Tula keeps only the fields you confirm, those don't stay in Tula unless you type them into a field.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone and work without the internet. They contain the deadline's title and what to submit.

How long we keep it

  • On your phone: until you delete the deadline, use Delete my data, or uninstall Tula.
  • In your account: until you delete the deadline or your account. Deleting a deadline removes its details from your account straight away; only a marker with its ID and the time it was deleted stays, so your other phones remove it too. Deleting your account removes everything, markers and error reports included, at once.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and to withdraw consent. Most of this you can do in the app: edit or delete any deadline, sign out, or delete everything from the You tab. For anything else, or a complaint, write to our grievance contact below. We reply within 7 days.

Deleting your data

In the app: You → Delete my data → Delete everything. If you can't open the app, see how to delete your Tula account.

Children

Tula is only for people aged 18 and over. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who sees it, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

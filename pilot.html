1. Install Tula

  2. On the page that opens, tap Install and download the app.
  3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then tap Install.

Tula isn't on the Play Store yet, which is why Android asks. The app comes from the Tula team's Expo account.

2. Set it up (3 minutes)

  1. Sign in on the You tab with your email. You'll get a 6-digit code; type it in. This backs up your deadlines and lets Tula read screenshots and PDFs.
  2. Save your first deadline: long-press a notice in a WhatsApp group, tap Share, then Tula. Check the card and tap Save.
  3. Allow notifications when Tula asks. Reminders are the whole point.
  4. Let Tula run in the background (next section). Without this, many phones hold reminders back to save battery.

3. Make sure reminders reach you

Find your phone brand. Menu names change a little between versions; if yours looks different, search Settings for "battery" and look for Tula. More detail for every brand: dontkillmyapp.com.

Samsung

Settings → Apps → Tula → Battery → Unrestricted

Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO

Settings → Apps → Manage apps → Tula → Battery saver → No restrictions

On the same screen, also turn on Autostart.

Realme, OPPO

Settings → Apps → App management → Tula → Battery usage → turn on Allow background activity and Allow auto launch

Vivo, iQOO

Settings → Battery → Background power consumption management → Tula → Allow

OnePlus

Settings → Apps → Tula → Battery usage → Allow background activity

Motorola, Google Pixel, Nothing, others

Settings → Apps → Tula → App battery usage → Unrestricted

Check it works: save a deadline marked "Matters" that's due 3 hours and 2 minutes from now, then close Tula. A reminder should arrive in about 2 minutes.

4. During the pilot

  • Send Tula every deadline you get: assignments, internship forms, hackathons, fees, society work. Text, screenshots and PDFs all work.
  • If Tula reads a date wrong, fix it on the card before saving. That tells us what to improve.
  • Every Friday we'll send a 2-minute form. It's the most useful thing you can do for us.
  • When we fix something, close and reopen Tula to get it; there's usually no need to reinstall.

Your data

  • Tula never reads your chats. It only sees what you share to it.
  • It keeps the fields you confirm, never the message or screenshot.
  • For the pilot we look at counts (how many deadlines you saved, how many you edited) and crash reports. Your deadlines are backed up to your account so your phones stay in sync; we don't look at them.
  • Delete everything any time: You tab → Delete my data.

Delete your Tula account

Tula is made by the Tula team. Deleting your account removes your email address, every deadline backed up to it, and the counts kept about your saves. Nothing is kept afterwards.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Tula and go to the You tab.
  2. Under Your data, tap Delete everything, then tap again to confirm.

This deletes your account and everything on your phone straight away, and cancels all reminders.

Without the app

Email [contact email] from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Tula account". We'll delete the account and everything in it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

Only want to remove some deadlines? Delete them one by one in the app's Deadlines tab; your account stays.

# Interview guide (20 minutes)

**Ask about what happened, not what they'd do.** "Tell me about the last time" beats "would you use". Don't mention Tula until the last question.

Bring: this guide, the notes sheet open, one of your three blurred notice screenshots sent to their phone just before you start.

## Before you start (1 min)

> Thanks for doing this. I'm trying to understand how college students keep track of deadlines: assignments, forms, internships, that kind of thing. There are no right answers, and I'm not selling anything. I'll take notes; is it OK if I also record, just for my own notes? It gets deleted after two weeks. And just to check, you're 18 or older?

If under 18: thank them, end there, don't record anything.

## Questions

Write their words, not your summary. Every row in the notes sheet has a column for each question.

### 1. The last miss (5 min, the most important part)

> Tell me about the last deadline you missed, or nearly missed. What was it, and what happened?

Probes, as needed:
- When did you realise? How?
- What were you doing in the days before?
- Had you seen the notice? Where was it?
- If they say "I never miss deadlines": *Tell me about the last one that was close.* If still nothing, note it and move on; that's data.

### 2. Where it came from

> Where did you first hear about it?

Listen for: class WhatsApp group, society group, Classroom, email, notice board, Unstop or Internshala, a friend, a professor in class.

### 3. Their groups

> How many WhatsApp groups are you in for college, societies and opportunities? Can you open WhatsApp and count with me?

Write the actual number. Ask which ones are muted.

### 4. Their system today

> How do you keep track of deadlines right now?

Probes: starred messages, screenshots, notes app, calendar, a to-do app, a friend who reminds you, nothing. *Show me.*

### 5. The screenshots folder

> Can you open your screenshots? Roughly how many from the last two weeks are notices you meant to act on?

Count together. Ask what happened to two or three of them.

### 6. The cost

> When you missed that one, what did it cost you?

Listen for: marks, an internship or offer, money (late fee), a team let down, stress. Note whether it can be undone.

### 7. Apps they've tried

> Have you ever tried an app for this? What happened? Why did you stop?

Ask specifically about: Todoist, TickTick, Google Calendar, MyStudyLife, Vidyantar, InterSemester, a WhatsApp reminder bot.

### 8. AI tools

> Do you use Gemini's student plan or ChatGPT for anything like this? What happens when you do?

### 9. The platform test (3 min)

Send them the screenshot of a real notice (blurred) on WhatsApp, then:

> Pretend this just came in your class group. Show me how you'd make sure you don't miss it, using whatever's on your phone.

Watch, don't help. Time it. Then:

> Is that good enough? Would you actually do that for every notice?

Write down: what they did, how long it took, whether the date came out right, and their answer to "good enough". This is the column that tells us whether the phone already solves this.

### 10. The forward (last, 2 min)

> Suppose you could forward any notice to a number, and it would make sure you act on it in time. What would you have forwarded this week?

Only now: *We're testing exactly that by hand for two weeks. Want to try it?* If yes, add them to the concierge with the welcome message in [messages.md](messages.md#welcome-individual).

## Close

> Thank you. Is there anyone else, ideally at a different college, who'd talk to me for 20 minutes?

## Listen for

- **Scattered sources:** "it was in some group", "I saw it but forgot where".
- **Specific losses**, with names of the form, company or subject.
- **Workarounds they built:** a notes file, a friend who reminds them, a group with just themselves.
- **Non-academic deadlines** (internships, hackathons, scholarships, fees) and whether they hurt more than coursework.
- **Already solved:** "my calendar handles it", "Gemini does this". Don't argue; write it down.

## Marking `scattered_info_miss`

Mark **Y** only if the story in question 1 (or a close call) happened because the notice was in one of many places and got lost or seen too late. Mark **N** if they missed it for another reason (knew, but ran out of time; forgot something they had written down). Mark **unclear** if you can't tell; unclear doesn't count toward the 10.

## Notice for the platform test, if you have none of your own

Use a real one from your groups wherever possible. If not, send this as a typed message:

> *[CSE-A] Internship drive: registration form closes Thursday 11:59 pm. Upload resume (PDF) + fill the form: forms.gle/... Late entries will NOT be considered. Pre-placement talk on 25/11, 10 am, Seminar Hall.*

It has two dates, one high-stakes form and a link, which is what makes saving it hard.

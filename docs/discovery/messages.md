## Concierge: individual number

### Welcome (individual)

Send when someone signs up. Don't add them until they reply YES.

> Hi [name]! Welcome to the Tula deadline concierge 👋
>
> How it works: when a notice with a deadline lands in any group, forward it here (text, screenshot or PDF). We'll reply with what we understood, and remind you before it's due: 3 days, 1 day and the morning of for forms, offers and fees; 1 day and 3 hours before for everything else.
>
> A real person (one of us) reads what you send. We only keep the deadline details, never your chats, and we delete everything on 30 Nov. Send STOP any time to leave.
>
> Please confirm you're 18+ and OK with this by replying YES.

### Got it (`/got`)

Reply within an hour of every forward, during waking hours.

> Got it ✅ **[title]** · due **[day, date, time]** · [what to submit, where].
> Reminders: [list of times]. Reply if anything's wrong.

### Date unclear

> Got it, but the notice says "[words, e.g. next week]". When's it actually due? I'll set the reminders once I know.

### Same as one you already sent

> Looks like the same deadline you sent on [day] ([title], due [date]). Kept as one, no extra reminders.

### Deadline moved

> Updated: **[title]** is now due **[new date, time]** (was [old date]). Old reminders cancelled, new ones: [times].

### Reminder (`/remind`)

> ⏰ **[title]** is due **[tomorrow 5 pm / in 3 hours / today 11:59 pm]**.
> [What to submit, where.] [Link, if any.]
> Reply DONE when it's in.

When they reply DONE:

> Nice, that one's handled 🙌

### STOP

> Done, you're out. We've deleted what you sent. Thanks for trying it!

## Concierge: class section

### Group setup

- WhatsApp group "**[Section] deadlines · Tula**", set to **only admins can send messages**.
- The class rep forwards notices to the section number in a private chat, not in the group.
- Group description: the profile description above, plus "Digest every evening at [time]. React ✅ to an item once you've done it."

### Evening digest (`/digest`)

Send at the agreed time every day, even when nothing new came in. Due-soonest first; everything due in the next 7 days.

> **[Section] · what's due** ([Day, date])
>
> 🔴 **Today**
> • [Title] · [time] · [what to submit, where]
>
> 🟠 **Tomorrow**
> • [Title] · [time] · [link]
>
> **This week**
> • [Day]: [Title] · [time]
> • [Day]: [Title] · [time] · *moved from [old day]*
>
> New since yesterday: [n]. React ✅ when you've done something.

If nothing is due: "Nothing due in the next 7 days. Enjoy it." Still send it, so people learn the digest is reliable.

### Thank the rep, daily

> Thanks for today's forwards! [n] went into tonight's digest.

## Surveys

### End-of-week check-in (individual number)

Send on Fri 20 and Fri 27. Five questions, about 2 minutes. A Google Form works; so does asking in the chat.

1. In the last 7 days, did you miss any deadline? If yes, which, and had you forwarded it?
2. How many notices did you *not* forward that you could have? Why not?
3. Which reminder was most useful, and was any too early, too late or annoying?
4. How would you feel if this concierge stopped tomorrow? *Very disappointed / Somewhat disappointed / Not disappointed*
5. Anything you'd change?

### Section poll (Fri 27)

A WhatsApp poll in the section group:

> Did the evening digest help you not miss something these two weeks?
> • Yes, it saved at least one deadline
> • Yes, it was useful but didn't save anything
> • Didn't make a difference
> • I didn't read it

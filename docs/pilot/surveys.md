# Pilot surveys

Three Google Forms. Turn on "Collect email addresses" so answers can be matched to Tula accounts, and add the same note at the top of each: *Answers are only seen by the two of us and are deleted when the pilot ends.*

## Intake (day 0, 1 minute)

The baseline the pilot is measured against.

| # | Question | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Which college and year are you in? | Short answer |
| 2 | Which phone brand do you use? (Samsung, Xiaomi/Redmi/POCO, Realme, OPPO, Vivo, OnePlus, Motorola, Google Pixel, Nothing, other) | Multiple choice |
| 3 | About how many WhatsApp groups are you in for college, societies and opportunities? | Number |
| 4 | In the last 4 weeks, how many deadlines did you miss or nearly miss? | 0 / 1 / 2–3 / 4 or more |
| 5 | What were they? (e.g. "internship form", "lab record") | Paragraph |
| 6 | How do you keep track of deadlines today? | Checkboxes: Nothing / Starred messages / Screenshots / Notes app / Calendar / To-do app / A friend reminds me / Other |
| 7 | I confirm I'm 18 or older. | Checkbox (required) |

## Weekly (every Friday, 2 minutes)

| # | Question | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | This week, did you miss or nearly miss any deadline? If yes, which one, and was it in Tula? | Paragraph |
| 2 | Roughly how many notices did you get this week that you could have put in Tula but didn't? Why not? | Paragraph |
| 3 | Did any reminder come too late, too early, or not at all? | Paragraph |
| 4 | Did Tula read a date or detail wrong? Paste the notice if you can (remove names first). | Paragraph |
| 5 | How would you feel if you could no longer use Tula? | Very disappointed / Somewhat disappointed / Not disappointed |

## Exit (end of week 4, 3 minutes)

| # | Question | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | How would you feel if you could no longer use Tula? | Very disappointed / Somewhat disappointed / Not disappointed |
| 2 | What is the main benefit you get from Tula? | Paragraph |
| 3 | In the last 4 weeks, how many deadlines did you miss or nearly miss? | 0 / 1 / 2–3 / 4 or more (same options as the intake, so they compare) |
| 4 | What kind of person do you think would benefit most from Tula? | Paragraph |
| 5 | How could we improve Tula for you? | Paragraph |
| 6 | Did you stop using Tula at some point? If yes, when and why? | Paragraph |
| 7 | Would you recommend Tula to a friend at another college? | 0–10 scale |

Question 1 of the exit survey is the "very disappointed" signal in the decision table; 40% or more is the target. Count it only for students who used Tula in at least 2 of the 4 weeks.

## Week 4: exit survey and interviews

```
The 4 weeks are up, thank you! Last form, 3 minutes: [exit form link]
Would you also do a 15-minute call this week? Reply with a time that works.
```

Exit interview prompts (15 minutes; ask about what happened, not what they'd do):

1. Walk me through the last deadline you saved in Tula. Where did it come from?
2. Was there a deadline you *didn't* put in Tula? Why not?
3. Did a reminder ever save you? Did one ever annoy you or come late?
4. What did you use before Tula? Would you go back to it?
5. *To students who stopped:* when did you stop, and what was going on that week?
6. If Tula disappeared tomorrow, what would you do instead?
